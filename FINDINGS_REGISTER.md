| **F-009** | Within the Reactive-JPY FX pair family, LSTM optimization dynamics differ substantially between Behavioral Surfaces. | **F-010** | Reactive-JPY LSTM Reference Benchmark: Using dataset v1.6.1 and reference training horizons (100 epochs for the Consensus Lifecycle Behavioral Surface; 1500 epochs for the Trend/Volatility Behavioral Surface), LSTM-derived prediction artifacts consistently improved out-of-sample adaptive trading performance within MPML relative to the no-DL baseline. Improvements were concentrated within the Reactive-JPY target family, while negative-control pairs showed near-zero or negative average Sharpe changes, supporting the hypothesis that the learned representations capture family-specific behavioral structure rather than broad market effects. Under the current MPML framework, the Consensus Lifecycle and Trend/Volatility Behavioral Surfaces produced comparable average downstream performance, suggesting they encode complementary rather than competing views of Reactive-JPY behavior. | MPML benchmark, dataset v1.6.1 | **Confirmed** |
| **F-011** | Persistent-family exits show substantial raw proximity to scheduled news, but this association is not supported as excess exposure after comparison with an empirical-calendar null. In P0C-3, 1,416 Persistent exits were analyzed; 61.79% had relevant news between the preceding and exit observations, with 75.92% within 6h, 89.76% within 12h, and 95.97% within 24h (median distance 2.58h). However, a 5,000-permutation empirical-calendar null preserving the observed interval durations and pair-specific news-calendar structure did not support excess association. News therefore remains a contextual variable tested in the commitment-lifecycle analysis rather than an established independent driver of Persistent termination. | P0C-3 Persistent news association; 5,000-permutation empirical-calendar null | **Confirmed** |
| F-012 | Persistent pair-specific downstream response structure is highly robust to predictive architecture. Across 17 Persistent behavioral configurations and five Persistent-family pairs, MLP and LSTM produce highly concordant downstream ΔSharpe responses. Architecture agreement is 0.917 by Pearson correlation and 0.865 by Spearman correlation across 85 pair × configuration observations, with the same direction of effect in 84/85 cases. The correlation between the five pair-level mean responses is 0.996. The result indicates that the dominant Persistent-family response structure is substantially architecture-invariant, while the magnitude and ranking of individual behavioral-state effects remain architecture-sensitive. | Persistent MLP/LSTM reference reports and architecture-agreement analysis | Confirmed
| F-013 | Persistent-family downstream response is strongly pair-specific rather than homogeneous across the five-pair population. Across the current 17 Persistent configurations, EURAUD and NZDUSD show consistently positive mean ΔSharpe responses, GBPUSD shows a smaller positive response, while EURGBP and EURUSD show negative mean responses. This structure is reproduced by both MLP and LSTM. The result indicates that Persistent-family aggregate performance can conceal substantial pair-level heterogeneity and that pair identity should remain explicit in subsequent Persistent analysis. | Persistent MLP/LSTM reference reports; baseline and downstream Persistent-family analysis | Confirmed
| F-014 | Persistent surface experiments preserve Reactive-JPY negative-control invariance. Across eight Persistent Trend/Volatility configurations spanning four Trend/Volatility environments and two feature sets, the Reactive-JPY control pairs EURJPY, GBPJPY, and USDJPY remain byte-identical in the corresponding per-pair outputs. This demonstrates that changing the Persistent representation perturbs the intended Persistent evaluation population without contaminating the non-Persistent control population. The result is an experimental-isolation finding and is not evidence that Persistent-family performance should be compared directly with Reactive-JPY control performance. | Persistent TrendVol diagnostic archive / per-pair walk-forward outputs | Confirmed |
